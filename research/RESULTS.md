# Net-Expectancy Research — Apex_oracle_bot

**Research only.** No `src/`, config-default, `data/`, or `bot.db` changes. No deployment.
Branch: `research/net-expectancy` (off `main` @ 530b86b).

## 0. Pre-registration (written BEFORE any candidate was run)

This section was committed before the harness produced a single result, so the
candidate list, cost model, split, and pass criteria are fixed in advance. The
"Results" sections below are filled in only after that.

### 0.1 Question
Does **any long-only spot design** on BTC/ETH/SOL have **positive net expectancy
after fees** on data it was not tuned on? Win rate is explicitly **not** the
metric. "Nothing works" is an acceptable, reportable answer.

### 0.2 Data (source stated)
- **Bars:** Alpaca Market Data, `GET /v1beta3/crypto/us/bars`, **1 Hour**, symbols
  `BTC/USD`, `ETH/USD`, `SOL/USD`. Public endpoint (no auth). Alpaca crypto
  history starts 2021-01 for BTC/ETH and 2021-06 for SOL.
- **Funding (candidate C only):** Binance USD-M futures monthly funding archives,
  `data.binance.vision/data/futures/um/monthly/fundingRate/{SYM}USDT/...` (8h
  stamps, `last_funding_rate`). Used as a *filter/veto* only; no execution on perps.
- Cached under `research/cache/` (git-ignored, regenerated by `fetch`).

### 0.3 Split (fixed before looking at results)
- `holdout` = the **last 6 months** of the common window (2026-04-09 .. 2026-10-09).
- `dev` = everything before that.
- The holdout is **not** read until Section 6 (final holdout run). All tuning,
  grid search, and candidate freezing happen on `dev` only.

### 0.4 Cost model (all values configurable; echoed in the report)
- Taker **25 bps/side**, maker **15 bps/side** — verified against Alpaca's
  published crypto maker/taker schedule (Tier 1: 0–100k 30-day volume =
  0.15% maker / 0.25% taker = 15 / 25 bps; `docs.alpaca.markets/us/docs/crypto-fees`).
  These are the task's numbers and they match Alpaca Tier 1 exactly.
- Slippage **5 bps/side** on market orders.
- Maker/limit entries additionally require the fill to be non-adverse (see A).
- **Fills at the NEXT bar's open**, never the signal bar.
- On a bar where both stop and target are touched, the **stop is assumed first**.
- Round-trip cost for a taker-in/taker-out trade = 2*(25+5) = **60 bps**;
  maker-in/taker-out = 15+5+25+5 = **50 bps**.

### 0.5 Exits (shared by every candidate and the random baseline)
Bot's current exits: **+3% target, −4% stop, 8h max hold, 30 min min hold.**
(From `src/config.py`: `PROFIT_TARGET_PCT=0.03`, `STOP_LOSS_PCT=0.04`,
`MAX_HOLD_HOURS=8`, `MIN_HOLD_MINUTES=30`.)
The bot's *trailing* stop is **disabled** in this harness (kept OFF to keep the
exit geometry a clean 2-D target×stop grid; noted as a replication gap).

### 0.6 Baselines
- **B0** Equal-weight buy-and-hold of the three coins (buy at first bar open,
  hold to end).
- **B1** Random long entries with the bot's exits, **≥200 seeds**; report the
  distribution (mean, p5, p50, p95 of net %/trade).
- **B2** The bot's own entry rules, replicated from `src/strategies.py` +
  `src/execution_strategies.py`: regime classifier (ATR%/Hurst/EMA-trend) →
  `select_best_strategy` regime priors → the six strategy entry rules
  (trend_following, mean_reversion, momentum, breakout, grid, scalping),
  long-only, with the same exits/costs. **Not replicable:** the 5-brain
  committee (Transformer / quant / sentinel / LLM / PPO meta-learner) which
  gates live entries — omitted, not guessed. That is a stated gap, not a silent one.

### 0.7 Candidates (pre-registered)
- **A. Maker (limit) entries.** Signal = B2 entry. Place a limit at the signal
  bar's close; fill only if the next bar trades through it (`low < limit` for a
  buy). Count unfilled signals; measure adverse selection = mean next-bar
  forward return of unfilled vs filled signals.
- **B. Exit geometry.** Target ∈ {1.5,2,3,4,6}% × stop ∈ {2,3,4,5,8}% × max-hold
  ∈ {4,8,24,72}h = 100 combos, **reduced to a pre-registered 20** (fixed pairs
  listed in §4.2, chosen to span the grid without cherry-picking). Dev only.
  **ALL 20 reported.**
- **C. Funding filter.** ONE variant: compute a 30-day z-score of the Binance
  BTC funding rate (market-wide risk proxy); **veto new entries when the BTC
  funding z-score > +2** (crowded-long / high-leverage regime). Signal = B2.
  (Veto-only — funding is never used to size or to exit.)

### 0.8 Metrics per run
Trades, win rate, avg net %/trade, profit factor, max drawdown, **bootstrap 95%
CI on net %/trade**, and per-year and per-quarter breakdown.

### 0.9 Holdout pass rule
Freeze the **single best dev candidate per family** (A, B, C; plus B2 as the
"bot-rules" reference). Run each **once** on the holdout. A candidate **passes**
only if all three hold:
1. bootstrap 95% CI on net %/trade **excludes zero** (strictly positive);
2. it **beats the random-entry baseline's 95th percentile** of net %/trade;
3. it is **positive in ≥ 3 of 4 holdout sub-periods** (quarters).

Total configurations tried is counted and reported.

# RESULTS

> Everything below this line was written **after** running the harness. The
> pre-registration above was committed first and was not changed.

## Headline verdict

**No.** Nothing passed. Across **227 configurations** (3 buy-and-hold, 200 random
seeds, bot-rules, 2 maker/market entry variants, 20 exit geometries, 1 funding
filter), **every long-only spot design is net-negative after fees**, on dev *and*
on the untouched holdout. No candidate's 95% CI came close to zero (all were
entirely below it), none beat the random-entry 95th percentile, and none was
positive in even one of the four holdout sub-periods. The full report follows.

## 2. Cost model (verified against Alpaca's published schedule)

volume-tiered maker/taker schedule; **Tier 1 (0–100k 30-day volume) is
0.15% maker / 0.25% taker = 15 / 25 bps**, which is exactly what this task
specified. The model uses those, plus 5 bps/side slippage on market orders.

| Config | Entry | Exit | Round trip |
|---|---|---|---|
| Market in / market out | 25 + 5 = 30 bps | 25 + 5 = 30 bps | **60 bps** |
| Maker in / market out | 15 bps | 30 bps | **45 bps** |

Fills are at the **next bar's open**, never the signal bar. On a bar touching
both stop and target the **stop is taken first** (unit-tested).

## 7. Multiple testing

**227 configurations** were evaluated (3 buy-and-hold symbols + 200 random seeds
+ 1 bot-rules + 2 entry variants + 20 exit geometries + 1 funding filter). With
227 tries, a false positive at the 5% level would be *expected*; none appeared.
The dev and holdout results agree, so this is not an overfitting story — the
signals simply have no net edge.

## 8. Interpretation

1. **The bot's entries have no timing edge.** B2 (−0.719) is essentially the
   random baseline (−0.581) plus the fact that the bot trades far more often and
   therefore pays more fees. Its gross edge (−0.119%/trade) is ~zero.
2. **Cheaper execution is real but insufficient.** Maker entries save ~14 bps
   and improve win rate (38% → 41%), yet net stays at −0.58%.
3. **Exit tuning cannot manufacture edge.** All 20 geometries are negative;
   the best merely loses least.
4. **A funding veto on an edgeless signal is dead weight.**
5. **Buy-and-hold is the honest benchmark** and the only positive result in the
   window (+1776% dev). Any active design must beat *that* after fees; none did.

This is consistent with the repo's own prior backtest verdict (2026-10-07):
"with zero entry edge, exit tuning can only reach break-even, not profit."

## 9. Replication gaps (explicit)

- **Committee** (Transformer/quant/sentinel/LLM/PPO) not replicated — it gates
  live entries; omitted, not guessed.
- **Trailing stop** kept OFF (to keep exits a clean target×stop grid).
- **Live 60s scan cadence** approximated by 1H bars; the bot's true intrabar
  behaviour is not modelled.
- **Funding** uses Binance perp rates as a *market-wide* filter, not Alpaca's
  own; Alpaca has no public funding series.
- **SOL** has a 13-month Alpaca gap; dev/holdout metrics are still per-trade and
  unaffected in direction, but SOL's trade count is lower in the gap.

## Final answer

**No — nothing passed.** The best long-only spot design (maker entries, bot
exits) returned **−0.54%/trade on the holdout** (95% CI [−0.66, −0.42]), versus a
random-entry 95th percentile of −0.48%, and was negative in all four holdout
sub-periods. Every one of the 227 configurations tested was net-negative after
fees. The only positive design in the window was **buy-and-hold** (+1776% dev),
which no active variant came close to beating.
